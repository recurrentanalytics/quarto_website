About

Timm Walker models climate risk for the systems people depend on: energy, water, food and infrastructure. Open data, transparent models, reproducible code.

About

I build open, reproducible models of climate risk for the systems people depend on, and learn in public how they can inform decisions.

From hazard data to decisions

  1. 01

    Models and data

    LiveLines, heat extremes, ERA5, IBTrACS, CLIMADA.

    → Showcase: Hurricane Maria 2017

  2. 02

    Early warning

    Flood warning in Vietnam, early action in the Mekong Delta and Latin America.

  3. 03

    Risk finance

    Parametric triggers for drought, flood and cyclones, with sovereign risk pools and humanitarian facilities.

    → Showcase: Trigger Lab

  4. 04

    Disaster risk management

    Critical infrastructure, business continuity, civil protection.

    → Showcase: The vanishing coast of the Mekong Delta

  5. 05

    People and institutions

    Monitoring and evaluation, workshops and trainings across languages.

Path

  1. 2014–2022

    development cooperation

    Nearly eight years in development cooperation taught me that risk is people, institutions and money.

  2. 2022–2026

    global reinsurer

    Four years in parametric insurance showed me how hazard data becomes a financial decision.

  3. now

    re:current_analytics

    LiveLines is where both meet.

Where

How I work: open data, readable assumptions, code anyone can run. Uncertainty is part of the result.

Contact: based in Hannover, working internationally, in German, English and Chinese. Open to research and public-sector roles:

Contact → LinkedIn

Email: timm.walker@recurrentanalytics.com

NoteShort bio

Timm Walker models climate risk for energy, water, food and infrastructure systems. He spent nearly eight years in development cooperation and four in parametric insurance, and builds open models such as LiveLines. He is based in Hannover, Germany.
