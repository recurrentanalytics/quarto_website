The papers, books, datasets, films, music and web sources cited on re:current_analytics: searchable and sortable, each with how far it was checked and links back to the pages that cite it.

The works this site cites, in one place: papers, books, data, films, music, and the articles and web pages it links as sources. Each entry links back to the pages that cite it and says how far its details could be checked.

    Bibliographic facts only, and only works that are publicly available. Entries with a DOI were checked against Crossref or DataCite; the others against the best source within reach: the publisher's, label's or provider's own page, a library or film catalogue, a review or, for a few, a reference site such as Wikipedia. Where a detail could not be confirmed, the entry says "partly checked"; where nothing could be checked (a page out of reach, or no edition named), "not checked". A mistake?
